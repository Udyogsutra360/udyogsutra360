Wastewater Treatment for Residential, Commercial & Institutional Applications.

What Is an STP?

A Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) treats wastewater generated from everyday human activities such as bathing, washing, cleaning, and sanitation facilities.

The treatment process removes harmful contaminants and organic matter so the treated water can be safely discharged or reused for flush tanks, gardening, and cooling towers. Treatment selection depends on flow capacity, site space, discharge standards, and energy preferences.

STP Applications

  • Residential Buildings & Housing Societies
  • Apartment Complexes & High-rises
  • Commercial Buildings & IT Parks
  • Hotels & Hospitality Resorts
  • Hospitals & Healthcare Centers
  • Educational Campuses & Universities
